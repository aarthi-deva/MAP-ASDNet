# Results

Recommended reproducibility outputs to commit:

- final held-out test metrics
- subject-level test predictions/probabilities
- five-fold summary metrics
- LOSO site-wise metrics
- ablation metrics
- McNemar raw and Holm-adjusted p-values
- validation-selected decision threshold(s)

Do not commit raw ABIDE-II data.

If subject IDs are considered inappropriate to expose under the source-data terms used by your institution, replace them with a stable study-specific pseudonymous identifier and provide a deterministic mapping procedure that does not redistribute protected or restricted information.
